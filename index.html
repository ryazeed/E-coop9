How a COOP term runs

Four stages every placement goes through, and who acts at each one.

  1. Company joins

    A company registers and the COOP office approves it before it can post.

  2. Opportunity posted

    The company posts a placement. It stays hidden until an admin approves it.

  3. Student placed

    Students apply, the company accepts, and the student joins the placement.

  4. Training and evaluation

    Daily check-ins, supervisor approvals, the final report and both evaluations close the term.

Built for every side of the placement

What students, companies, academic supervisors and the COOP office can each do.

Students

  • Browse and filter approved opportunities
  • Apply and track application status
  • Check in with the daily code
  • Submit the final training report

Companies

  • Post opportunities for admin approval
  • Accept or reject applicants
  • Generate the daily attendance code
  • Evaluate each trainee

Academic supervisors

  • Monitor assigned students
  • Approve or reject attendance
  • Review final reports
  • Submit academic evaluations

COOP office

  • Approve companies and opportunities
  • Manage and suspend accounts
  • Assign academic supervisors
  • View analytics and the audit log
